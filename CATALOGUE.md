# 24 tangible problems worth solving

Three draft briefs in each of eight domains. These are bounded starting points beneath the broader [problem map](PROBLEMS.md), not a ranking of all human needs.

**Read the targets correctly:** every number is a proposed pilot acceptance threshold. No local baseline, partner commitment or achieved result is asserted. See [methodology](METHODOLOGY.md). Draft status is deliberate.

## Choose a first contribution

- **You have lived experience:** challenge the problem definition and what success should mean.
- **You operate a workflow:** supply an anonymised baseline and the current best comparator.
- **You build:** choose one brief, declare access constraints and test the smallest uncertain assumption.
- **You commission work:** provide a host, a bounded budget and an independent acceptance test.
- **You evaluate:** improve the metric, expose confounding or publish a negative result.

No confirmed commissioners or pilots yet. Propose one through a pull request or issue.
