# What would make life better?

A starting list of 24 problems people might recognise in their own lives.

The voices below are illustrative, not research quotes. These are draft choices that need review by people living in different circumstances. Each link opens the measurable brief.

## Afford the basics

A decent life should leave room for more than survival.

### [Afford a safe home with money left to live](problems/essentials/PWS-0201.md)

*“I want somewhere decent to live without giving up everything else.”*

A stable home near work, school and people you care about, with enough income left for food, savings and enjoyment.

### [Eat well without worrying about the grocery bill](problems/essentials/PWS-0202.md)

*“I want to feed my family properly and still have money at the end of the week.”*

Food that is nourishing, enjoyable and culturally familiar, without excessive cost, cooking time or waste.

### [Keep the lights on and the home comfortable without bill shock](problems/essentials/PWS-0203.md)

*“I should not have to choose between a comfortable home and paying the bill.”*

Reliable electricity, tolerable indoor temperatures and predictable bills, including for people who rent.

## Feel well

Pain, illness and exhaustion should not consume the life we have.

### [Get the right help when something feels wrong](problems/health/PWS-0204.md)

*“I want to know what is happening to me without months of worry and repeated appointments.”*

A person reaches appropriate care, understands the next step and avoids being lost between providers.

### [Have more days when pain does not decide what you can do](problems/health/PWS-0205.md)

*“I want to walk, work, sleep and enjoy things without pain running my life.”*

Meaningful improvement in the activities a person chooses, alongside affordable access to appropriate care.

### [Wake up rested enough to enjoy the day](problems/health/PWS-0206.md)

*“I do not want to spend every day exhausted.”*

Better daytime energy and functioning, with support that fits real work and family schedules.

## Feel financially secure

A setback should not wipe out years of effort.

### [Earn a dependable living without losing your whole life to work](problems/security/PWS-0207.md)

*“I want to earn enough, be treated fairly and know when I can be home.”*

Work that produces a sustainable income, predictable hours and room for family and rest.

### [Survive an unexpected bill without a debt spiral](problems/security/PWS-0208.md)

*“One broken appliance or medical bill should not undo years of trying.”*

A household can absorb a setback, keep essentials paid and recover without unaffordable borrowing.

### [Keep your savings safe from scams](problems/security/PWS-0209.md)

*“I should not lose my money because someone knew how to frighten or fool me.”*

People can use digital services confidently, spot suspicious requests and get help before irreversible loss.

## Have time to live

Our days should contain time we get to choose.

### [Get some of your day back from chores](problems/time/PWS-0210.md)

*“After work and chores, I want some energy left for myself.”*

Less repetitive household work and a fairer division of what remains.

### [Spend less of your life travelling to work](problems/time/PWS-0211.md)

*“I want to get home while there is still some day left.”*

Shorter, more predictable and affordable journeys, with less exhaustion and more time at home.

### [Finish essential paperwork without losing a day](problems/time/PWS-0212.md)

*“I want to sort out a form, claim or renewal without taking time off work.”*

People receive the service or correct resolution they need with less time, cost and confusion.

## Care for the people we love

Caring should not require abandoning our own lives.

### [Find childcare you trust and can afford](problems/care/PWS-0213.md)

*“I want to work or rest knowing my child is safe and cared for.”*

Reliable care that suits a family's real schedule, supports the child and does not consume an impossible share of income.

### [Help ageing parents live with dignity without constant family worry](problems/care/PWS-0214.md)

*“I want them to keep their independence, and I want to know when they need help.”*

Older people retain control over daily life while families and carers coordinate dependable support.

### [Have time and energy for the people you love](problems/care/PWS-0215.md)

*“I do not want the people I love to get only what is left of me.”*

More unhurried, wanted time together, without one person carrying all the planning.

## Have a fair chance

Where we start should not determine everything we can become.

### [Help every child learn without feeling left behind](problems/learning/PWS-0216.md)

*“I want my child to understand, feel capable and keep wanting to learn.”*

A child gains understanding and confidence, with support their family can access.

### [Start again when your work no longer offers a future](problems/learning/PWS-0217.md)

*“I want a realistic way forward, even if I cannot afford to stop earning.”*

An adult can gain a usable skill and move towards better work without unaffordable study costs or false promises.

### [Be understood wherever life takes you](problems/learning/PWS-0218.md)

*“I want to ask for help and understand the answer, even when I do not speak the language.”*

People can manage everyday situations with dignity and less dependence on someone else to speak for them.

## Feel connected and at peace

We should have people to turn to and space to feel at ease.

### [Have someone you can turn to](problems/belonging/PWS-0219.md)

*“I want someone I can call when things go well—or when they do not.”*

More mutual, dependable human relationships and less unwanted isolation.

### [Get support when worry feels too heavy](problems/belonging/PWS-0220.md)

*“I want help before I feel completely overwhelmed.”*

People can reach appropriate, affordable human support, feel understood and regain some control over daily life.

### [Disagree without every conversation becoming a fight](problems/belonging/PWS-0221.md)

*“I want us to understand each other without hurting each other.”*

People can express needs, recognise disagreement and resolve practical issues while retaining their own judgment.

## Live somewhere safe and healthy

The place we call home should support a good life.

### [Breathe cleaner air where you live and work](problems/neighbourhood/PWS-0222.md)

*“I do not want the place I live to make me or my children unwell.”*

Less harmful exposure in homes, schools and workplaces, without pushing costs or pollution onto someone else.

### [Trust the water coming into your home](problems/neighbourhood/PWS-0223.md)

*“I want safe water when I turn on the tap, without wondering what it might do to my family.”*

Dependable access to verified safe water, with less fetching, expense and uncertainty.

### [Keep your family safe when extreme weather reaches your street](problems/neighbourhood/PWS-0224.md)

*“I want to know what to do, where to go and how to keep the people I love safe.”*

Families receive understandable, actionable help in time, including those who cannot simply drive away or stay indoors.

## How to read these problems

Every brief contains three measurable parts, an explanation of AI's possible role, limits and risks, and a first experiment. A small successful experiment is a step towards the ambition, not proof the whole problem is solved.

All 24 are drafts. No measured baseline, confirmed commissioner or achieved benefit is claimed. [Read the measurement rules](METHODOLOGY.md).

## Whose everyday life?

We use ordinary language, but there is no single average person. A stable salaried household, a farmer, an older person living alone and a family displaced by conflict have different constraints. We need their input, not assumptions that one product fits everyone.
