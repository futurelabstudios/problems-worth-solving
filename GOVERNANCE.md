# Governance

The purpose of governance is to keep the list trustworthy, plural and useful.

## Roles

### Problem steward

Maintains one entry, incorporates evidence and publishes progress or setbacks.

### Domain reviewer

Tests evidence, causal reasoning, feasibility and prior work.

### Community reviewer

Represents people directly affected by the problem. Lived experience is evidence, not decoration.

### Safety reviewer

Examines misuse, rights, exclusion, privacy, reversibility and second-order effects.

### Maintainer

Applies repository standards, manages review and protects the integrity of the process.

### Commissioner

Provides authority, resources, access, data or an environment in which work can be tested. Commissioners do not own the truth.

## Decision process

A seed entry may begin as a draft. Promotion to **active** normally requires:

- one domain review;
- one maintainer approval;
- a named steward;
- a defined outcome and next step;
- review by people affected by the problem, including whether the proposed benefit matters to them;
- evidence of how the benefit will reach those people;
- and safety review where material.

Material conflicts of interest must be disclosed. Financial supporters may fund work but cannot buy rankings, suppress evidence or prevent the publication of negative results.

## Statuses

- **Draft** — incomplete and open for specification.
- **Seed** — credible initial entry seeking review and stewardship.
- **Active** — sufficiently specified, maintained and open for commissioning.
- **In progress** — at least one declared intervention is underway.
- **Solved locally** — the outcome was achieved in a defined context.
- **Archived** — superseded, disproven, inactive or unsafe.

“Globally solved” should be used extremely rarely.

## Disagreement

Reasonable people will disagree about importance, causality and acceptable risk. Preserve the disagreement when it changes the decision. Minority views supported by evidence may be documented rather than forced into artificial consensus.

## Appeals

A rejected or archived entry may be reconsidered through an issue that identifies:

- the disputed decision;
- the evidence or reasoning believed to be wrong;
- and any new evidence.

## Removal

Entries may be archived or, where safety or privacy requires, removed. Version history should otherwise remain visible so that failure and learning are not erased.

## Governance development

This is a provisional structure. As participation grows, governance should move beyond a single organisation toward a credible council including domain experts, commissioners, builders and people living with the problems.
