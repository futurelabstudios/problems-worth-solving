# PWS-0117: Check whether a cited source actually supports a claim

[Catalogue](../../CATALOGUE.md) · [Grand challenge map](../../PROBLEMS.md) · Parent challenge: 6.2

> **Draft challenge specification.** Targets are proposed pilot thresholds, not measured results or forecasts. Baseline, feasibility and affected-user review remain open. No pilot, partner, funding or achieved benefit is claimed.

## The ordinary human experience

A report looks convincing because every paragraph has a link. The links may not say what the report claims.

## First-principles decomposition

A citation must match the claim's population, date, magnitude and causal strength. Source existence is weaker than support.

The three separately testable constraints are specified below. A gain in one does not compensate for failure in another.

## Why AI changes what is possible

Break prose into atomic claims, retrieve cited passages and highlight unsupported steps. Compare with keyword search and analyst review.

## System boundary

Public business and science summaries with accessible sources; no universal truth score or automated censorship.

## Measurable sub-problems

All current numerical baselines are **unknown** for the proposed host. Establish them using the same denominator and sampling rules before an intervention. The targets below apply to the first pilot's scope only.

| Sub-problem | Baseline to establish | Proposed pilot target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identify unsupported citations | Current workflow on the same eligible cases | ≥90% precision and ≥80% recall | Blinded expert labels on held-out claim-source pairs. |
| Reduce checking time | Current workflow on the same eligible cases | 40% less analyst time per verified claim | Include reading disputed passages and resolving errors. |
| Expose uncertainty | Current workflow on the same eligible cases | Every unresolved claim visibly marked | Unavailable sources remain unverifiable, never silently passed. |

## Target rationale and value estimate

These thresholds are editorial design choices: large enough to test practical usefulness, without claiming to estimate an achievable effect from existing evidence. They must be revised after baseline collection and host review. Error thresholds are provisional engineering gates, not a certification of safety.

Estimate value from observed volume × attributable improvement × value per outcome, subtracting data preparation, integrations, inference, human review, maintenance and failure costs. Use the units above; do not add money, hours and quality into a single score. See [measurement rules](../../METHODOLOGY.md).

## Evidence and prior attempts

Fact-checking, citation tools and source search. Humans still judge disputed interpretations and source quality.

**Evidence status:** The specific failure mechanism and effect size require local validation. Prior approaches named here are comparators to investigate, not an exhaustive literature review. No verified local baseline or successful AI pilot is available in this entry.

## Data and infrastructure

Licensed or openly accessible documents and claim-source annotations. Preserve source versions and access dates.

**Available now:** This repository supplies the specification only. It does not supply a dataset or grant access. A host must confirm lawful access, coverage and data quality before the experiment.

## Builders and commissioners

Publishers, research teams, procurement teams and professional-service firms.

These are plausible commissioning routes, not confirmed partners. The first steward should recruit both an operational owner and affected users before setting deployment criteria.

## Risks, rights and failure modes

Correct quotation may still come from a false source. Separate support, source credibility and factual truth; report each independently.

## Real-world success and evaluation design

Use all three rows as a joint acceptance contract; improving one metric alone is insufficient. When the pilot measures a proxy, such as a reconciled list or a successful simulation, do not describe it as a demonstrated improvement in mortality, employment, relationships or mission survival.

Build 300 claim-source pairs across three subjects, test on a held-out subject and run a crossover with analysts over six weeks.

Lock the eligibility rules, comparison workflow and evaluation data before inspecting results. Publish all attempted cases, corrections, dropouts and failures. Report uncertainty and subgroup outcomes where meaningful. Small pilots estimate feasibility and variance; they do not prove rare-event safety or population-wide effects.

## Stop or redesign if

Stop if false accusations of unsupported claims consume more review effort than the system saves.

## Global transfer

New languages and subject areas require fresh expert evaluation, especially where causal language differs.

## Review and prioritisation

**Unscored draft.** Assigning precise impact or feasibility scores before baseline and specialist review would imply knowledge we do not yet have. Promote to seed or active only through [governance](../../GOVERNANCE.md), with documented evidence and ownership.
